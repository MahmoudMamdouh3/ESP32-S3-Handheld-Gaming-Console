The 3 Pillars of Your Handheld Console

  1. Hardware Interface (firmware/BmoGameboy/src/core/): Talks to your soldered screen (ST7789), 12 input buttons, SD card reader, and animated BMO expressions.
  2. 14 Console Engines (src/emulators/ & src/engine/): Standalone emulator cores (GB, GBC, NES, SMS, PCE, Atari, Pico-8, Genesis, SNES, WonderSwan, NGP, Lynx, Coleco, DOOM).
  3. Safety Guardrails & Simulator (tools/guardian/ & tools/bmo_simulator/): Automated test suite verifying memory limits before flashing, plus the web playtester and 3D enclosure CAD.
